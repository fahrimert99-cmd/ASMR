```
*(68 karakter)*

**Açıklama:**
```
Muscle cramp, eat a banana. The evidence behind that advice is far weaker than most people think, and what a banana actually does inside a muscle cell is more interesting than the myth.

About ninety eight percent of your body's potassium is already inside your cells, so a banana is not refilling an empty tank. This video follows it from ripeness and digestion to the sodium potassium pump that burns roughly a fifth of your resting energy, the ninety millivolt charge it maintains, and the moment a contraction fires and resets.

The key idea: potassium does not cause the contraction. Potassium is what makes the next one possible. It is not the spark. It is the reload.

What Avocado Really Does in Your Gut (Why Fat Free Dressing Wastes Your Salad)
```
*(77 karakter)*

**Açıklama:**
```
The most powerful thing an avocado does has almost nothing to do with the avocado. It has to do with everything else on your plate.

Carotenoids, the pigments that make carrots orange and tomatoes red, are fat soluble. Without fat there are no micelles to carry them, so they travel the length of your intestine, past every absorbing cell, and leave your body untouched. You ate them. You never absorbed them.

This video follows the whole route: how fat triggers bile release, how bile salts build micelles, why those micelles are the only way a fat soluble pigment reaches the intestinal wall, and why the packaged result takes the lymphatic system instead of going straight to the liver.

CHAPTERS

4:50 Four rules for eating avocado

THE FOUR RULES
1. Do not eat avocado alone if you can eat it with vegetables.
2. Stop using fat free dressing.
3. Do not throw away the dark green flesh under the peel.
4. Half an avocado is enough. This is not an argument for eating four.

ON THE MICROBIOME
The fibre in avocado does shift the bacterial community, and that has been measured in randomised trials. Those changes are real and they are modest. Anyone promising a transformed microbiome from one fruit is selling something.

DISCLAIMER
This video explains biological mechanisms. It is not medical advice. Avocado is energy dense, and if you have a latex allergy it is one of the fruits that can cross react.
